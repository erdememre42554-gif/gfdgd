Legal

Privacy policy

Effective 10 September 2026. This policy describes what ParseFrame does with data — which, in practice, is very little.

1. Scope

This policy covers the public website at parseframe.app and every tool published on it. It does not cover third-party sites you reach by following a link.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, store or process: your files; file names; file contents; text you paste; image pixels; colour values; page counts; dimensions; passwords; or any document metadata. The tools operate on data inside your browser and the site has no endpoint that accepts it.

3. Data processed locally

Aggregate event counts (for example, that a tool was opened) are stored in your browser's localStorage under the key pf:aggregate:v1. They are not transmitted. You can delete them at any time by clearing site data for this domain. No cookies are set by the site.

4. Server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like any web server, it processes request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested path and timestamp in order to deliver the page and protect the service. This metadata never includes the contents of a file you are working on, because your file is never part of a request.

5. Third parties

  • Hosting / CDN: delivers the static build.
  • Unsplash: training articles display photographs hotlinked from Unsplash's image CDN, as their API guidelines require. Your browser requests those images directly from Unsplash, which may see your IP address and user agent.
  • No analytics, advertising, tag manager or session-recording service is used.

6. Legal bases (where GDPR applies)

Serving the site relies on a legitimate interest in operating a functional website. Local aggregate counts are stored on your own device and are not personal data in our hands. We do not build profiles and do not make automated decisions about you.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from tool use, there is usually nothing to access, correct or erase on our side. If you have corresponded with us by email, you may ask for that correspondence to be deleted. Requests: hello@parseframe.app.

8. Children

The site is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

Material changes to this policy are recorded in the changelog with a new effective date.
